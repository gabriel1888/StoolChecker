Privacy

How this site handles information

This page explains the intended information handling for the checker and optional contact forms. It should be completed with the site owner’s contact and retention information before launch.

Checker selections

When you choose a stool type or urine colour, the selection and the resulting general information are processed in your browser. The checker is designed not to use app-managed storage for these selections, including accounts, local storage, or app cookies.

This does not mean that every part of the wider delivery path is known to this page. For example, the website host may process technical request information as described below.

Optional Tally forms

No Tally form is loaded until you select a load button or use the direct Tally link. If you choose to load or visit a form, Tally receives the contact details you decide to submit and processes its own technical data in connection with providing the form.

Those form interactions are handled by Tally under its own practices. Do not submit information you are not comfortable sharing through that third-party form.

Website hosting and logs

The hosting provider may collect technical information in server or security logs, such as IP address, browser or device details, request times, and the pages requested. The precise data, purpose, recipients, and retention depend on the hosting configuration and must be confirmed by the site owner.

Owner details still required

Privacy contact: To be supplied by the site owner before launch.

Retention information: To be supplied by the site owner before launch, including any arrangements for Tally submissions and hosting logs.

If you have a privacy question before these details are published, please do not use the optional contact forms.
